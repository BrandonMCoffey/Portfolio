Following the critical acclaim of the [UTD Vivarium Digital Twin](/projects/vivarium)—winner of the **2025 Gold Davey Award for Best World-Building Design**—the **Vivarium Guided Tour** expansion transforms the static architectural model into an interactive self-guided training application.

### Interactive Pedagogical Waypoints

New laboratory staff must master complex gowning, airlock sequencing, and bio-containment procedures before ever entering the physical cleanroom:

- **Guided Teleportation Nodes**: Guided pathing takes users through the exact sequential order required when transitioning between anterooms, sterile surgical suites, and animal holding wards.
- **Audio Hotspots & Knowledge Checks**: Synchronized audio narration from veterinary directors explaining facility pressure gradients, cage sanitization autoclave cycles, and personal protective equipment (PPE) requirements.
- **Compliance Quizzing**: Interactive 3D checkpoints verifying protocol retention directly within the simulated laboratory environment.
