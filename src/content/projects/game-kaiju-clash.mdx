**Kaiju Clash** is an asymmetric multiplayer action game built in **Unity 6 (`6000.3.16f1`, URP)** alongside teammates Sai Mangipudi, Michael Cabrera, and Chris Gauthier, pitting teams of agile first-person Hunters against a player-controlled giant Kaiju across a destructible urban skyline.

### Hunter Traversal, Tracking & Orbital Laser Signals

- **High-Mobility Traversal**: Hunters navigate vertically across skyscrapers using grappling hooks (`GrappleAbility`), jetpack fuel (`FuelController`), and launch pads (`HunterLaunchPad`).
- **Behemoth Tracker & 1D Compass (`CompassManager`)**: Sound-reactive sonar pings alert Hunters on a 1D compass HUD whenever the Kaiju generates noise through footsteps, swings, stomps, stampedes, or obliteration wind-ups.
- **Orbital Laser Signals (`OLS_WeaponThrowable`)**: Hunters retrieve throwable beacons from POI stations and stick them onto the Kaiju to call down a tracking orbital beam, shattering the Kaiju's outer shell and exposing weak points.

### Kaiju Destruction & Multiplayer Systems

- **Kaiju Combat & City Destruction**: The Kaiju wields shockwaves (`KaijuShockwave`), obliteration attacks (`KaijuObliterate`), and doom escalation (`KaijuDoomData`) against procedurally placed destructible buildings (`CityGenerator`, `BuildingScatter`, `DestructibleManager`).
- **Unity 6 Netcode & Services**: Powered by **Unity Netcode for GameObjects**, **Unity Gaming Services Multiplayer**, **Vivox** voice chat, **VFX Graph**, **Animation Rigging**, and custom `NetworkVisibilityManager` and `ScenePhysicsManager` optimization systems.
