Kaiju Clash is an asymmetric multiplayer action game built in Unity 6 alongside teammates Sai Mangipudi, Michael Cabrera, and Chris Gauthier, pitting teams of agile first-person Hunters against a player-controlled giant Kaiju across a destructible urban skyline.

### Hunter Traversal, Tracking & Orbital Laser Signals

- High-Mobility Traversal: Hunters navigate vertically across skyscrapers using grappling hooks, jetpacks, and launch pads.
- Behemoth Tracker & 1D Compass: Sound-reactive sonar pings alert Hunters on a 1D compass HUD whenever the Kaiju generates noise through footsteps, swings, stomps, stampedes, or obliteration wind-ups.
- Orbital Laser Signals: Hunters retrieve throwable beacons from POI stations and stick them onto the Kaiju to call down a tracking orbital beam, shattering the Kaiju's outer shell and exposing weak points.

### Kaiju Destruction & Multiplayer Systems

- Kaiju Combat & City Destruction: The Kaiju wields shockwaves, obliteration attacks, and doom escalation against procedurally placed destructible buildings.
- Unity 6 Netcode & Services: Powered by Unity Netcode for GameObjects, Unity Gaming Services Multiplayer, Vivox voice chat, VFX Graph, Animation Rigging, and custom network visibility and scene physics optimization systems.
