Developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) for the **Ackerman Center for Holocaust Studies**, this project reconstructs the physical center's research archives, lecture spaces, and museum exhibits into an accessible, interactive digital twin. Originally launched in 2025 and actively migrated in 2026 to an in-house **Unity 6 (`6000.6.2f1`, URP)** WebGL and desktop platform, it serves as an international educational bridge allowing students, researchers, and visitors to engage in guided tours, lectures, and historical exploration.

### Spatial Archival Preservation & Multimedia Exhibits

The Ackerman Center holds unique archival records, rare survivor testimony collections, and historical artifacts digitized into interactive 3D displays:

- **Archival Cabinet & Artifact Inspection (`ZsuszannaCabinet`)**: Interactive exhibition cabinets—including the Zsuzsanna Ozsváth collection—with animated doors, camera focus overrides, and shelf-level item inspection.
- **Synchronized Audio & Transcripts (`TextAudioSync`)**: Word-level synchronized narration and text highlighting for readings such as _When the Danube Ran Red_, paired with `TableOfContentsNavigator`, `AccordianSection`, and `QuizUIHandler` learning modules.
- **Networked Lectures & Slideshows (`SlideShow`)**: Synchronized presentation screens allowing instructors and guides to step through slides live with online visitors.

### In-House Metaverse Architecture

Built on VAST Lab's reusable Unity 6 packages—[`vast-metaverse-tools`](https://github.com/VAST-Lab/vast-metaverse-tools) and [`vast-metaverse-networking`](https://github.com/VAST-Lab/vast-metaverse-networking)—the platform supports **Unity Netcode for GameObjects** multiplayer, **UniVRM** avatars, **Vivox 3D spatial voice chat** with camera-aware panning and speaking indicators, and **UGS Lobby & Relay** matchmaking for WebGL (Desktop/Mobile) and PC.
