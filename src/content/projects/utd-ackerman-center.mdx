Developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) for the Ackerman Center for Holocaust Studies, this project reconstructs the physical center's research archives, lecture spaces, and museum exhibits into an accessible, interactive digital twin. Originally launched in 2025 and actively migrated in 2026 to an in-house Unity 6 WebGL and desktop platform, it serves as an international educational bridge allowing students, researchers, and visitors to engage in guided tours, lectures, and historical exploration.

### Spatial Archival Preservation & Multimedia Exhibits

The Ackerman Center holds archival records, survivor testimony collections, and historical artifacts digitized into interactive 3D displays:

- Archival Cabinet & Artifact Inspection: Interactive exhibition cabinets—including the Zsuzsanna Ozsváth collection—feature animated doors, camera focus transitions, and shelf-level artifact inspection.
- Synchronized Audio & Transcripts: Word-level synchronized narration and text highlighting accompany readings such as _When the Danube Ran Red_, paired with table-of-contents navigation, expandable sections, and comprehension quizzes.
- Networked Lectures & Slideshows: Synchronized presentation screens allow instructors and guides to step through slides live with online visitors.

### In-House Metaverse Architecture

Built on VAST Lab's shared Unity 6 metaverse package suite—[vast-metaverse-tools](https://github.com/VAST-Lab/vast-metaverse-tools) and [vast-metaverse-networking](https://github.com/VAST-Lab/vast-metaverse-networking) (the same foundation powering the [ATEC Campus Digital Twin](/projects/utd-atc-building), [SPN Gallery](/projects/utd-spn-gallery), and [Gemstone Gallery](/projects/vast-gemstone-scanning))—the platform supports Unity Netcode for GameObjects multiplayer, UniVRM avatars, Vivox 3D spatial voice chat with camera-aware panning and speaking indicators, and UGS Lobby and Relay matchmaking for WebGL and desktop.
