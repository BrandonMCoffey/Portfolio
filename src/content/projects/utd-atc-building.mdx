The **UTD Campus Digital Twin** is an accurate, highly-optimized interactive virtual reconstruction of the iconic Edith O’Donnell Arts and Technology (ATEC) building at The University of Texas at Dallas. Hosted live on [Spatial.io](https://www.spatial.io/s/UTD-School-of-Arts-Humanities-and-Technology-65e7bba43cbbbf6b6bb4b0e9?share=5810302130767391610), the environment runs seamlessly in-browser on mobile devices, desktop browsers, and standalone VR headsets without requiring local installs.

### Cross-Platform Optimization & WebXR Performance

Recreating a 155,000-square-foot multi-story university complex within Spatial.io's strict WebGL memory budgets required aggressive technical art optimization:

- **Geometry & Draw Call Budgeting**: Modular architectural kitbashing with atlased PBR textures, reducing multi-room rendering overhead to under 80 draw calls.
- **Progressive Lightmap Baking**: High-resolution indirect illumination and caustic reflections baked into HDR lightmaps via Unity's Progressive Lightmapper.
- **Interactive Multi-User Hubs**: Integrated student gallery spaces, virtual lecture auditoriums, and collaborative breakout zones supporting synchronous multi-user voice and avatar interaction.
