The **Holocaust VR Experience** is an educational historical documentary developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) in close consultation with Holocaust historians and educators at the **Ackerman Center for Holocaust Studies**. The project leverages immersive virtual reality to bring archival photographs, survivor testimonies, and historical artifacts into a solemn spatial context.

### Ethical VR Design & Sensitive Storytelling

Designing immersive historical narratives around traumatic 20th-century history demands deep ethical care:

- **Spatial Restraint**: Prioritizing historical artifacts, audio testimonies, and solemn environments over sensationalized dramatization.
- **Atmospheric Lighting Direction**: Utilizing Unreal Engine 5's Lumen illumination to sculpt quiet, contemplative light-and-shadow volumes that mirror the dignity of survivor oral histories.
- **Archival Photogrammetry**: Digitizing physical historical artifacts from university archives into 3D assets that students can inspect with natural head movement.

### Educational Deployment

Deployed across university classrooms and museum workshops, the application provides educators with a powerful pedagogical tool to foster historical empathy, counter disinformation, and preserve eyewitness memories for future generations.
