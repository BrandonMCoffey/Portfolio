Developed in collaboration with **Arkenstone Rare Minerals**, this project pioneers specialized technical art and computer vision workflows to digitize, reconstruct, and interactively render ultra-rare mineral specimens, gemstones, and crystalline structures in Unreal Engine 5.

### The Challenge of Scanning Translucent Crystals

Standard photogrammetry algorithms fail on minerals due to internal light refractions, specular caustics, and total internal reflection:

- **Cross-Polarized Lighting Array**: Custom camera rigs utilizing linear polarizing filters on both strobe illumination and camera lenses to eliminate surface glare and isolate true volumetric pigmentation.
- **Micro-Photogrammetry**: Macro-focus stacking pipelines capturing crystalline facets with sub-millimeter geometric precision.

### Custom Physically Based HLSL Shading

Real-time gemstone rendering demands custom optical shader calculations beyond standard PBR:

- **Wavelength-Dependent Chromatic Dispersion**: Simulating Abbe number refraction splits in real-time ray-marched pixel shaders.
- **Birefringence (Double Refraction)**: Reproducing the anisotropic optical splitting characteristic of calcite, tourmaline, and quartz crystal structures.
- **Thin-Film Iridescence**: Implementing spectral wave interference equations for iridescent mineral veneers and peacock ore.
