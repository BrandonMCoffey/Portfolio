**OpenWater Sports** (*Riversports*) is a competitive multiplayer water sports game developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) in partnership with **OKC Riversport** in Oklahoma City. Built in Unity 6 (`6000.3.16f1`, URP), the game brings accessible, physics-driven rowing and whitewater kayaking to WebGL, PC, and mobile players to build public engagement ahead of the **2028 Olympics**.

### Playable Events & Water Physics

- **Rowing Race (`RR-OklahomaRiver`)**: Competitive sprint rowing along buoy-lined lanes on the Oklahoma River (`RacingShellController`, `BouyLanes`), balancing stroke timing, stamina, and wake dynamics.
- **Whitewater Kayaking (`WW-RiversportOKC`)**: Slalom and rapids navigation (`KayakController`, `KayakGate`) featuring turbulent flow currents (`RiverZone`, `CurrentZone`) and obstacle avoidance.
- **WaterWorks Hydrodynamics**: Custom buoyancy, wave displacement, and cross-platform shader fallbacks (`SwapMaterialOnWebGl`) tailored for both desktop and browser WebGL performance.

### Dedicated Server Multiplayer & Customization

- **Cross-Platform Networking**: Built on **Unity Netcode for GameObjects** and **Unity Gaming Services (UGS)** (`LobbyNetworkManager`, `MatchmakingManager`, `LeaderboardManager`) with support for Unity Dedicated Server hosting alongside WebGL, Windows PC, and Android clients.
- **Boathouse & Athlete Customization**: Interactive avatar and watercraft customization (`CustomizeAvatarPanel`, `CustomizeVehiclePage`), Olympic country flag selection, Animation Rigging paddle IK (`PlayerIKManager`), and mobile touch HUD controls (`MobileControls`).
