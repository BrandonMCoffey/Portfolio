**OpenWater Sports** is a competitive multiplayer game experience developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) in partnership with **OKC Riversport** in Oklahoma City. The system brings semi-realistic kayak and rowing movements into a web-playable and accessible gaming experience to bring excitement and interest to the world of Olympic water sports.

The game is still a work-in-progress, and this page will be updated as development continues.
