OpenWater Sports is a competitive multiplayer water sports game developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) in partnership with OKC Riversport in Oklahoma City. Built in Unity 6, the game brings accessible, physics-driven rowing and whitewater kayaking to web, PC, and mobile players to build public engagement ahead of the 2028 Olympics.

### Playable Events & Water Physics

- Rowing Race: Competitive sprint rowing along buoy-lined lanes on a virtual Oklahoma River course, balancing stroke timing, stamina, and wake dynamics.
- Whitewater Kayaking: Slalom and rapids navigation modeled after the RIVERSPORT OKC whitewater channel, combining directional river current zones with gate navigation and obstacle avoidance.
- Hydrodynamic Foundation: Shared watercraft physics handle buoyancy, wave displacement, and automatic material fallbacks so the water rendering runs cleanly in both desktop and WebGL browser builds.

### Dedicated Server Multiplayer & Customization

- Cross-Platform Networking: Built on Unity Netcode for GameObjects and Unity Gaming Services (matchmaking, lobbies, and leaderboards), supporting dedicated server hosting alongside WebGL, Windows PC, and Android clients.
- Boathouse & Athlete Customization: A persistent profile and boathouse flow lets players customize avatars, Olympic country flags, and watercraft, paired with inverse-kinematics paddle animation and adaptive mobile touch controls.
