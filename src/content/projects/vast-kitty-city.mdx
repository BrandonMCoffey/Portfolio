**Kitty City** is an interactive Augmented Reality experience installed at the [New Mexico Museum of Art — Vladem Contemporary](https://www.nmartmuseum.org/vladem-contemporary/) in Santa Fe, inspired by [Judy Chicago's iconic artwork](https://judychicago.com/gallery/kitty-city/kc-artwork-2/). As Lead Developer at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab), I spearheaded the technical pipeline to translate stylized 3D feline character models, blend shapes, and locomotion animations into Unity for passthrough augmented reality on the Meta Quest Pro and Quest 3.

### Technical Pipeline: Rigging & Maya-to-Unity Automation

Exporting complex multi-joint character rigs with squash-and-stretch controls required a custom Maya Python/Qt toolchain to bake FK/IK constraints into clean skeletal hierarchies before engine ingestion. Learn more about the DCC automation on the [Maya Python & Qt Tools project page](/projects/maya-python-qt-tools).

### Spatial Anchors & Physical Alignment

To enable virtual cats to navigate gallery benches, archways, and pedestals naturally, an exact 1:1 digital twin floor plan of the Vladem Contemporary exhibition space was constructed. Meta Spatial Anchors were pinned to physical architectural datum points, ensuring persistent, drift-free room alignment across multi-day museum operations.
