**Kitty City** is an interactive Augmented Reality experience installed at the [New Mexico Museum of Art — Vladem Contemporary](https://www.nmartmuseum.org/vladem-contemporary/) in Santa Fe, inspired by [Judy Chicago's iconic artwork](https://judychicago.com/gallery/kitty-city/kc-artwork-2/). As Lead Developer at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab), I spearheaded the technical pipeline to translate stylized 3D feline character models, blend shapes, and locomotion animations into Unity for passthrough augmented reality on the Meta Quest Pro and Quest 3.

### Technical Pipeline: Rigging & Maya-to-Unity Automation

Exporting complex multi-joint character rigs with squash-and-stretch controls required a custom Maya Python/Qt toolchain to bake FK/IK constraints into clean skeletal hierarchies before engine ingestion. Learn more about the DCC automation on the [Maya Python & Qt Tools project page](/projects/solo-maya-python-qt-tools).

### Spatial Anchors, AI Behaviors & Physical Alignment

To enable virtual cats to navigate gallery benches, archways, and pedestals naturally, an exact 1:1 digital twin floor plan of the Vladem Contemporary exhibition space was constructed with runtime navigation mesh baking. Meta Spatial Anchors were pinned to physical architectural datum points to align the virtual gallery with the physical museum floor.

Autonomous feline behavior is driven by state machines and weighted action probabilities, paired with blendshape animation, hand-gesture beckoning and touch reactions, gallery boundary hazard warnings, and inspector tooling powered by [Coffey Utils](/projects/solo-coffey-utils).
