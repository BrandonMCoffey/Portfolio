Developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) for the UT Dallas SPN Gallery, this project reconstructs the gallery exhibition space as an accessible, interactive digital twin playable directly in the browser via [itch.io](https://vastlab.itch.io/utd-spn-gallery).

### Shared Unity 6 Metaverse Architecture

Like the [Ackerman Center Digital Twin](/projects/utd-ackerman-center), [UTD Campus Digital Twin](/projects/utd-atc-building), and [Gemstone Gallery](/projects/vast-gemstone-scanning), the SPN Gallery is supported by VAST Lab's shared Unity 6 UPM packages:

- [vast-metaverse-tools](https://github.com/VAST-Lab/vast-metaverse-tools): Third-person character controller and camera rig, runtime UniVRM avatar importing with automatic shader repair, interactive object inspection, seating triggers, quest tracking, and cross-platform WebGL and desktop input.
- [vast-metaverse-networking](https://github.com/VAST-Lab/vast-metaverse-networking): Unity Netcode for GameObjects synchronization, UGS Lobby and Relay matchmaking, networked player nametags, and Vivox 3D positional voice chat for guided gallery tours.
