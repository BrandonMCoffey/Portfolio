The **VR 9/11 Memorial** is a standalone virtual reality historical commemoration experience developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) for the **UT Dallas Military and Veterans Center**. Built in **Unity 6 (`6000.3.16f1`, URP)** for Meta Quest headsets, the experience honors the victims, first responders, and service members connected to the events of September 11, 2001.

### Three-Chapter Historical Experience

Orchestrated by a timed narrative sequence (`SceneTransitionManager`, `FadeScreenVisual`) with synchronized audio narration and screen fades, visitors progress through three distinct historical scenes:

1. **World Trade Center (`WorldTradeCenter`)**: A spatial reconstruction of the Twin Towers plaza complex prior to the attacks.
2. **Ground Zero Aftermath (`DestroyedWorldTradeCenter`)**: A solemn post-event environment depicting the destroyed World Trade Center site with atmospheric smoke and emergency vehicles.
3. **Rubble with Flag (`RubbleWithFlag`)**: A commemorative tribute scene honoring the iconic flag raising at Ground Zero.

### Spatial Narrative & Standalone Quest Optimization

- **Floating Spatial Annotations (`FloatingTextManager`)**: Dynamically anchors reflective historical narratives and timeline quotes around the viewer's head space with smooth distance, scale, and fade distribution.
- **XR Interaction & Hand Tracking**: Built on OpenXR, **XR Interaction Toolkit 3.4.1**, and **Unity XR Hands 1.7.3** to support both tracked controllers and natural bare-hand interaction.
- **Meta Quest Performance**: Tuned for standalone Android XR exhibition hardware with `FixedFoveationSetup` (fixed foveated rendering) and custom URP optimization passes.
