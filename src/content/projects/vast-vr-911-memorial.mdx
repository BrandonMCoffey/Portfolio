The **VR 9/11 Memorial** is an immersive virtual reality historical commemoration experience developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) for the **UT Dallas Military and Veterans Center**. Built in the Unity Game Engine, the project honors the victims, first responders, and service members connected to the events of September 11, 2001.

### Community Exhibition & Impact

- **Exhibition Date**: September 2026
- **Location**: Dallas, Texas
- **Showcase Feature**: [LinkedIn Memorial Feature](https://lnkd.in/p/g9zV4AaH)
