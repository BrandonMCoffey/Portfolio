The VR 9/11 Memorial is a standalone virtual reality historical commemoration experience developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) for the UT Dallas Military and Veterans Center. Built in Unity 6 for Meta Quest headsets, the experience honors the victims, first responders, and service members connected to the events of September 11, 2001.

### Three-Chapter Historical Experience

The application is structured around a continuous, timed narrative controller that blends audio narration, lighting transitions, and cross-faded scene loading across three historical chapters:

1. World Trade Center: A spatial reconstruction of the Twin Towers plaza complex prior to the attacks.
2. Ground Zero Aftermath: A post-event environment depicting the destroyed World Trade Center site with atmospheric smoke and emergency vehicles.
3. Rubble with Flag: A commemorative tribute scene honoring the iconic flag raising at Ground Zero.

### Spatial Narrative & Standalone Quest Optimization

- Floating Spatial Annotations: A dynamic text manager anchors historical quotes and narrative reflections around the viewer's head space with controlled spacing, scale, and fade timing.
- XR Interaction & Hand Tracking: Built on OpenXR, Unity XR Interaction Toolkit, and XR Hands to support both tracked controllers and natural bare-hand interaction.
- Meta Quest Performance: Tuned for standalone Android XR exhibition hardware with fixed foveated rendering and optimized URP lighting and geometry passes.
