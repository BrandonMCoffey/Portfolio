The **VR 9/11 Memorial** is an immersive virtual reality historical commemoration experience developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) for the **UT Dallas Military and Veterans Center**. Built in Unreal Engine 5, the project honors the victims, first responders, and service members connected to the events of September 11, 2001.

### Architectural Reconstruction & Solemn Atmosphere

The experience focuses on contemplative, solemn exploration. Utilizing Unreal Engine 5's Nanite geometry virtualization and Lumen global illumination, the environment faithfully recreates commemorative architectural monuments, reflective water pools, and bronze memorial parapets with deep physical material accuracy.

### Spatial Audio & Experiential Pacing

A major focus was placed on acoustic design:

- **Directional 3D Audio**: Gentle water cascade frequencies dynamically attenuate across the plazas, creating pockets of quiet reflection.
- **Guided Reflection Mode**: Visitors can engage with ambient spoken oral histories or explore the grounds in complete silence at their own pace.
