Developed at the [Multimodal Interaction Lab (MI Lab)](/projects?filter=milab), the **SciVis Multi-Sensory Visualization Suite** explored novel paradigms for perceiving complex multi-dimensional scientific data. By pairing immersive stereoscopic VR with mid-air ultrasound radiation pressure arrays and micro-thermal feedback actuators, researchers can literally feel volumetric scalar fields, temperature gradients, and turbulent flow boundaries without wearing physical gloves or holding mechanical controllers.

### Mid-Air Acoustic Focal Point Modulation

The system leverages an **Ultraleap phased transducer array**, driving hundreds of ultrasonic speakers to construct focused acoustic pressure points in free space. By modulating carrier wave frequencies in real time:

- Users perceive tactile textures, distinct vibration frequencies, and surface boundaries hovering in mid-air.
- Hand-tracking cameras continuously map bare fingertips to virtual data isosurfaces, steering focal points dynamically to follow the user's hand gestures.

### Thermo-Tactile Multi-Modal Fusion

To visualize multi-variate datasets (such as atmospheric heat flux or thermodynamics):

- Peltier thermoelectric elements were synchronized with the spatial rendering engine, applying rapid heating and cooling cycles alongside ultrasound vibrations.
- This multi-sensory feedback significantly reduced cognitive error rates when test subjects identified critical thresholds within dense 3D scalar fields.
