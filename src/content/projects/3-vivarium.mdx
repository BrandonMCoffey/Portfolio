Developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) in partnership with the **UT Dallas Office of Research and Innovation**, the **UTD Vivarium Digital Twin** is an accurate real-time architectural and operational simulation of the university's biomedical animal research and quarantine laboratories. Built in Unreal Engine 5, the digital twin serves as a remote facility orientation, regulatory compliance training platform, and institutional showcase.

### Architectural Precision & Specialized Equipment

Biomedical research vivariums require rigorous bio-containment protocols, directional airflow systems, sterilizers, and specialized airlocks. Every corridor, sanitization station, animal housing rack, and surgical preparation suite was reconstructed from architectural CAD schematics with millimeter accuracy.

### Real-Time Lighting & Material Fidelity

Leveraging Unreal Engine 5's **Lumen** dynamic global illumination and custom PBR materials authoring in Substance, the environment faithfully captures the harsh fluorescent illumination, antimicrobial epoxy floor reflections, and stainless steel surface roughness characteristic of sterile cleanrooms.

### Interactive Guided Training & Tour Expansion

Following the completion of the core digital twin, an interactive guided pedagogical walkthrough was developed, allowing prospective researchers and inspection officials to explore the vivarium without breaking physical sterile barriers.
