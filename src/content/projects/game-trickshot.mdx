Trickshot is a deck-building arcade basketball game built in Unity where players assemble rosters, draft hands of action cards, and chain dribbles, passes, and dunks to entertain the crowd and build hype.

### Card-Driven Trickshots & Crowd Hype

- Action Deck Foundation: A modular card and hand manager lets players draw from customizable decks, drag and reorder cards in a curved hand layout animated with DOTween, and queue chained trickshot sequences.
- Hype Scoring & Crowd Response: Routines are evaluated dynamically against Bronze, Silver, and Gold hype tiers, rewarding creative card synergies with escalating crowd reactions and visual previews.
- Dynamic Camera Framing: Cinemachine virtual cameras blend smoothly between wide court views and multi-target trickshot angles that track the ball and athletes mid-play.

### Roster Progression & Game Modes

- Team Loadout & Economy: Players earn currency from shows to unlock, buy, and assign player cards across court positions, backed by persistent local save data.
- Multi-Mode Structure: Organized across separate scenes for roster and shop management, an interactive tutorial, the main halftime performance mode, and a dribble minigame, tuned for both Android mobile and PC.
