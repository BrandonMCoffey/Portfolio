**Trickshot** (*Basketball Tricks*) is a deck-building arcade basketball game built in Unity 2022.3 (URP) where players assemble rosters, draft hands of action cards, and chain dribbles, passes, and high-flying dunks to entertain the crowd and maximize hype.

### Card-Driven Trickshots & Crowd Hype

- **Action Deck System (`ActionDeckManager`)**: Players draw from customizable decks, drag and reorder action cards in a curved hand layout powered by DOTween, and execute chained trickshot sequences.
- **Hype Scoring Tiers**: Routines are scored dynamically against Bronze, Silver, and Gold hype thresholds (`HypeScoreDisplay`, `CrowdController`), rewarding creative card synergies and crowd excitement.
- **Dynamic Trickshot Camera (`TrickshotCamera`)**: Smoothly blends Cinemachine virtual cameras between court overview and dynamic multi-target trickshot angles as the ball and players move.

### Roster Management & Game Modes

- **Team Loadout & Progression (`GameManager`, `GameSaveData`)**: Players earn currency from performances to unlock, purchase, and assign player cards across court positions with persistent local save progression.
- **Multi-Scene Experience**: Features `MainUI` for team and shop management, an interactive `Tutorial`, the main `HalftimeShow` performance mode, and a `DribbleGame` minigame, tuned for both Android mobile (with Samsung Adaptive Performance) and PC.
