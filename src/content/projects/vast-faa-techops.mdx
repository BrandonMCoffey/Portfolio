The **FAA Technical Operations Extended Reality (XR) Comfort Analysis & Maintenance Simulation** is a safety-critical training and ergonomics research system developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) in partnership with the **Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)**. The platform provides Augmented Reality and Mixed Reality applications to evaluate wearability, comfort, and cybersickness—helping determine safe wear durations and break intervals through technical operations training scenarios.

### Human Factors & Multi-Device User Studies

The research spans multiple hardware platforms and dedicated IRB user-study software across two repositories:

- **HoloLens 2 MRTK3 & OpenXR Study Suite ([`FAA-HololensDemo`](https://github.com/VAST-Lab/FAA-HololensDemo))**: Built in Unity 2022.3 with **MRTK3**, **Microsoft Mixed Reality OpenXR**, and **glTFast**. Runs structured study sessions (`UserStudy`, `StartUserStudyManager`, `UserStudyLiveController`, `StudyPlayback`) with a companion tablet interface (`UserStudyTabletInterface`), QR code spatial alignment (`QRCodeDetection`), and timed in-headset questionnaires tracking **Cybersickness** and **General Headset Discomfort** (`CybersicknessQuestions`).
- **Vuzix M4000 Native Android Study ([`FAA_Vuzix_UserStudy`](https://github.com/VAST-Lab/FAA_Vuzix_UserStudy))**: Native **Android Studio (Java)** application using the **Vuzix Speech Recognition SDK** (`VuzixSpeechClient`), Bluetooth SPP tablet pairing, and JSON-driven audio narration and subtitles (`instructions.json`) to guide technicians hands-free through forklift assembly, multimeter probing, and UPS procedures via voice commands.

### Modules & Simulations

Across the AR and MR applications, technicians and study participants complete procedural and spatial tasks:

1. **Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) Bypass & Maintenance (`BypassProcedure`, `UPSInteractions`)**: Step-by-step Kirk Key and breaker lock-out/tag-out protocols with animated hand guidance and error feedback.
2. **Digital Multimeter Calibration (`MultimeterInteractions`)**: Interactive probe placement, dial hints, voltage testing, and circuit measurements.
3. **Air Traffic Control Plane Routing (`AirTrafficScenarioManager`)**: Interactive 3D aircraft pathing and routing adjustments (`PlanePathDraggablePoint`).
4. **Spatial Manipulation & Forklift Assembly (`BlockGameScenario`)**: Precision spatial block-placement tasks and forklift assembly guidance.
