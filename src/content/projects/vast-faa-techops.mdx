The FAA Technical Operations Extended Reality (XR) Comfort Analysis and Maintenance Simulation is a safety-critical training and ergonomics research platform developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) in partnership with the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA). Using Augmented and Mixed Reality applications across several head-mounted displays, the system evaluates wearability, physical comfort, and cybersickness to help establish safe wear durations and break schedules during aviation maintenance tasks.

### Human Factors & Multi-Device User Studies

The software architecture supports controlled IRB user studies across both optical see-through mixed reality headsets and monocular smart glasses:

- HoloLens 2 MRTK3 & OpenXR Study Suite: Built in Unity with MRTK3, Microsoft Mixed Reality OpenXR, and glTFast. A structured session flow pairs the headset application with a companion tablet controller, QR code spatial alignment for table calibration, study playback tools, and timed in-headset questionnaires that record cybersickness and general headset discomfort ratings.
- Vuzix M4000 Native Android Study: Built in Android Studio using Java, the Vuzix Speech Recognition SDK, and Bluetooth SPP tablet pairing. A data-driven step sequencer plays synchronized audio narration, subtitles, and reference diagrams while letting technicians navigate forklift assembly, multimeter probing, and UPS procedures hands-free via voice commands.

### Training Modules & Scenarios

Across the AR and MR applications, participants work through modular procedural and spatial tasks:

1. Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) Maintenance: Step-by-step Kirk Key and breaker lock-out/tag-out procedures with animated hand guidance, flipbook instructions, and error feedback.
2. Digital Multimeter Calibration: Interactive probe placement, dial guidance, voltage testing, and circuit measurements.
3. Air Traffic Control Plane Routing: Spatial 3D aircraft pathing and routing adjustments over a virtual airfield.
4. Spatial Manipulation & Forklift Assembly: Precision block-placement tasks and guided hardware assembly scenarios.
