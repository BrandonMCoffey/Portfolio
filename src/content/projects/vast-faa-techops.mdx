The **FAA Technical Operations Extended Reality (XR) Maintenance Suite** is a safety-critical training and ergonomics research system developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) in close partnership with the **Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)**. The platform simulates high-voltage airway facilities equipment—including Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS), air traffic communication towers, and navigational aid maintenance procedures—using spatial mixed reality headsets.

### Human Factors & Wear Time Research

Beyond technical procedural guidance, the project investigates cognitive load, physical neck/eye strain, and spatial interaction degradation across sustained XR usage in field maintenance environments:

- **Ergonomic Fatigue**: Measuring cervical spine tilt angles and center-of-mass head torque during multi-hour overhead equipment inspections.
- **Cognitive Aptitude Tailoring**: Adapting UI guidance density based on real-time task completion heuristics and eye-gaze tracking metrics.

### Procedural Modules & Hardware Simulation

The suite trains field technicians across several high-risk operational modules:

1. **Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) Maintenance**: Step-by-step breaker lock-out/tag-out (LOTO) protocols and internal capacitor discharge sequencing.
2. **Digital Multimeter Calibration**: Realistic probe interactions, voltage testing, and live multimeter circuit measurements.
3. **Air Traffic Control Tower Infrastructure**: High-altitude navigation beacon maintenance and forklift safety maneuvers.
