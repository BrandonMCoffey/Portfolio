The **FAA Technical Operations Extended Reality (XR) Comfort Analysis & Maintenance Simulation** is a safety-critical training and ergonomics research system developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) in close partnership with the **Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)**. The platform provides Augmented Reality and Mixed Reality applications to test wearability, comfort, and cybersickness to recommend how long users can wear the XR headsets, how often breaks are needed, and how long they should last. This is facilitated through equipment training on operations such as a Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS), routing airplane traffic over a tower, multimeter usage, and other small training scenarios and procedures.

### Human Factors & Wear Time Research

Beyond technical procedural guidance, the project investigates cognitive load, physical neck/eye strain, and spatial interaction degradation across sustained XR usage in field maintenance environments:

- **Ergonomic Fatigue**: Measuring cervical spine tilt angles and center-of-mass head torque during multi-hour overhead equipment inspections.
- **Cognitive Aptitude Tailoring**: Adapting UI guidance density based on real-time task completion heuristics and eye-gaze tracking metrics.

### Modules & Simulations

The suite trains field technicians across several operational modules:

1. **Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) Maintenance**: Step-by-step breaker lock-out/tag-out protocols.
2. **Digital Multimeter Calibration**: Multimeter probe interactions, voltage testing, and circuit measurements.
3. **Air Traffic Control Plane Routing**: Simplification of airplane re-routing and navigation adjustments.
