**The Woodbearers of Chimayo VR** is an immersive virtual reality museum installation developed by the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) for the [New Mexico Museum of Art — Vladem Contemporary](https://www.nmartmuseum.org/vladem-contemporary/). The project reconstructs Clark Hulings' masterwork _Woodbearers of Chimayo_ (1974) into a living, explorable 3D historical environment.

Visitors step beyond the picture frame into 1970s Chimayó, New Mexico, observing the wood-bearing donkeys, adobe architecture of the Santuario de Chimayó, and atmospheric lighting from any vantage point.

<ImageEmbed file="original_painting.jpg" caption="Clark Hulings' original 1974 masterwork: 'Woodbearers of Chimayo'" />

### Custom Stylized Post-Processing Shader

A crucial technical requirement was translating Hulings' impasto brushwork, edge dry-brushing, and warm gouache/oil color grading into a real-time post-process volume in Unreal Engine 5. Using custom HLSL scene texture sampling, Sobel edge filtering, and normal-based canvas paper texturing, the rendering seamlessly captures the tactile quality of a painted canvas:
