The **Volumetric Video Capture & Playback Pipeline** is an ongoing research and development initiative at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) investigating cutting-edge techniques to ingest, compress, and render 4D holographic video captures of human performers in real-time game engines.

### Evercoast Integration & Multi-Camera Ingestion

In partnership with industry volumetric capture platform **Evercoast**, the research pipeline processes multi-camera synchronized RGB-D video arrays, reconstructing continuous photorealistic 3D point clouds and dynamic mesh sequences.

### Real-Time Playback & 4D Gaussian Splatting

Traditional volumetric video produces immense per-frame mesh data, straining GPU memory bandwidth. This project investigates:

- **Compute Shader Mesh Decoding**: Custom GPU compute pipelines that decompress temporal delta geometry directly in VRAM.
- **4D Gaussian Splatting**: Exploring neural radiance fields and splatting representations for volumetric actors with view-dependent specular highlights and natural hair transparency.
- **Pedagogical Tooling**: Developing user-friendly Unity and Unreal Engine editor importer plugins so undergraduate animation and theatre students can integrate live holographic acting into interactive productions.
