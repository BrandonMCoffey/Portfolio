The **OKC Riversport XR Simulation** is an interactive virtual reality training experience developed at the [VAST Lab](/projects?filter=vastlab) in partnership with **OKC Riversport** in Oklahoma City. The system brings the adrenaline, hydraulic forces, and navigational challenges of Class II–IV artificial whitewater channels into an immersive headset simulation.

### Hydrodynamic Simulation & Kayak Buoyancy

Accurately capturing kayak behavior in turbulent rapids required custom physics modeling:

- **Buoyancy Raymarching**: Submerged hull volume sampling to calculate dynamic buoyant center-of-mass forces across moving wave crests and hydraulic holes.
- **Eddy & Current Vectors**: Directional vector fields mapping river eddies, boils, and downstream surge velocities.

### Physics-Based Paddle Propulsion

Using tracked VR motion controllers with custom paddle peripheral attachments:

- Fluid drag equations calculate resistive paddle blade thrust based on blade immersion angle and sweep velocity.
- Real-time resistance curves provide natural haptic cues, training athletes on effective forward strokes, draw strokes, and roll maneuvers without water risk.
