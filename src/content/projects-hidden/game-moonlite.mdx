Moonlite is a 2D sci-fi extraction roguelike and shooter built in Unity alongside teammates Mikey Bess and Sai Mangipudi. Players descend onto hazardous moons, mine rare mineral veins through destructible terrain, synthesize specialized elemental ammunition, and extract back to the mother ship before alien swarms overwhelm them.

### Mineral-Synthesized Elemental Ammunition

Each mined mineral powers a distinct projectile behavior configured through modular data assets and an object-pooled bullet system:

- **Copper**: High-velocity standard rounds
- **Cryselium**: Freezes enemies on impact
- **Voltthorn**: Chains lightning arcs between nearby targets
- **Phaseum**: Pierces enemies and phases through terrain walls
- **Pyrokite**: Incendiary rounds inflicting burn damage over time
- **Aethergem**: Penetrates foes and destroys terrain blocks
- **Corestone**: Heavy bouncing kinetic projectiles

### Mother Ship Hub & Planetary Expeditions

- Spaceship Hub: Between runs, players return to the mother ship to access the research lab, weapon forge, upgrade menus, inventory management, and mission navigation.
- Planetary Mining & Extraction: Expeditions take place across destructible 2D terrain backed by quad-tree spatial queries, dynamic flashlight lighting, automated mining stations, enemy swarms, and emergency extraction teleporters.
- Core Systems: Built on a modular Unity 2D foundation with pooled audio management, JSON-based save persistence, flexible mouse and controller UI switching, and Cinemachine camera feedback.
